# Customising Flexium without a web developer

Everything in this guide is done from the Moodle site, with the mouse. You do not need to write code, edit files
or know CSS. You need a site administrator account.

There are two ways to change Flexium:

- **Magic wands, on the page itself.** Turn *Edit mode* on (top right). A small wand button appears next to each
  part you can change. Click it to open its options in a side panel. Changes show on the page at once, but nobody
  else sees them until you click *Save*. *Discard changes* puts everything back.
- **Theme settings**, in *Site administration > Appearance > Themes > Flexium*. Use them for colours, fonts and
  images, which apply to the whole site.

Most catalogue options are in both places.

## Course catalogue (*Courses* page)

Open *Courses* with *Edit mode* on. The wands are on the following parts.

### Page heading
- Show or hide the page heading.
- Write your own heading text.

### Breadcrumbs
- Turn breadcrumbs on or off.
- Choose the pages where they appear.
- Choose what they show: a short path, or Moodle's full path.
- Choose the home item: an icon, text, or none.
- Set the maximum depth and the maximum length of each item.
- Hide the current page, course categories or course sections.
- Choose the separator (›, /, →, · or |).
- Choose the style (plain, pills or boxed) and what small screens show.

### Filters button (left edge)
- Choose the icon (funnel, sliders, compass, and more).
- Choose the colour, the size and the height on the page.

### Blocks button (right edge)
- Choose the icon, or keep Moodle's own.
- Choose the colour, or keep Moodle's grey.
- Choose the size and the height on the page.

### Filters panel
- **Which filters to show, and in what order.** Drag them, or use the arrows. Filters can be course categories
  (shown as a tree), course tags, or any course custom field.
- Rename each filter and choose whether it starts expanded.
- Show or hide:
  - "Expand all / Collapse all";
  - course counts next to each option;
  - options that have no courses;
  - a search box inside long filter groups;
  - a "Clear" button for each group;
  - a start-date filter.
- Choose whether the panel opens by default.
- Choose whether each user's open or closed groups are remembered.

### Quick filters
- Choose the one-click buttons above the filters: *My courses*, *Favourites*, *New*, *Starting soon*, *In
  progress* and *Open enrolment*.
- Put them in your order, and give each one your own name.

### Catalogue header
- Add a banner above the results, with your title and text.
- Choose one of six colour styles for it.

### Results toolbar
- Choose the default order: name, start date, end date, latest courses or popularity.
- Show or hide the "share this search" link.
- Show or hide the loading placeholders.

### Grid and list views
- Offer both views, or only one.
- Choose the default view.
- Choose the number of columns in the grid (automatic, 2, 3 or 4).
- Give the list view its own card content: image size, and whether it shows the category, summary, badges and card
  footer.

### Course cards (the wands are on the first card)
- **Image:**
  - Choose the status badges (enrolled, completed, new, starting soon, favourite).
  - Show or hide the progress bar and the favourite star.
  - Turn the quick view on or off, and choose its format: a centred window, a panel from the right or the whole
    screen.
  - Choose the header of courses without an image: one of six colour designs, a different colour for each course,
    Moodle's pattern, or an image you upload.
- **Texts:** show or hide the category and the summary.
- **Badges:**
  - Choose which course custom fields and tags appear as badges, and in what order.
  - Choose a colour for each kind of badge.
  - Show one line per kind of badge.
- **Card footer:** choose what the bottom of the card shows (start date, end date, teachers, number of participants,
  custom fields, and more) and in what order.

### Courses per page
- At the bottom of the results, choose how many courses a page shows.
- Choose whether users can change that number themselves.

### All settings
The *All settings* button in the editor bar lists every catalogue option in one place. Some options have no wand of
their own and are only here:
- the "Catalogue" link in the top menu (on or off, and its text);
- the course page shown to users who are not enrolled.

## Site home page

Open the site home page with *Edit mode* on. The page is made of sections.

- **Start from a template.** Click *Templates* and pick one of ten designs: Academy, Catalogue first, Community,
  Compact, Corporate, Webinars and events, Minimal, Public sector, Skills or University.
- **Edit a section.** Click its wand and change its texts, buttons, colours, images and options.
- **Add a section.** Click **+** between two sections. Available sections:
  - Hero, with an optional course search box;
  - Courses: latest, popular, starting soon, from a category, or chosen by you;
  - Categories;
  - Figures: numbers of courses, users and categories;
  - Feature boxes;
  - Steps;
  - Testimonials;
  - Partners (logos);
  - Questions and answers;
  - News;
  - Call to action;
  - Text;
  - Space;
  - Moodle content: Moodle's own site home items and blocks.
- **Arrange sections.** Move them up or down, duplicate them, or delete them.

## Site footer

Every page shows the footer. With *Edit mode* on, the footer has its own editor bar.

- **Footer style.** Use the wand in the footer bar to choose:
  - the background (dark, light, your brand colour or a colour design);
  - the alignment;
  - whether to show a bottom bar;
  - whether to show Moodle's footer button.
- **Columns.** Add up to six columns, move them, or remove them.
- **Blocks.** Add blocks to any column or to the bottom bar, then edit each one with its wand:
  - text;
  - links;
  - contact details;
  - social networks;
  - logo;
  - legal links;
  - copyright;
  - "Contact site support";
  - login information;
  - "Reset user tour".
- **Move blocks.** Drag a block, or use its arrows to move it within or between columns.

By default the footer shows the site's contact email and address, the main links and the site summary.

## Page margins

With *Edit mode* on, the *Page margins* button at the bottom left sets the empty space on the left and right of the
pages.

- Choose where it applies: the whole site, the home page or the catalogue.
- Set both sides together, or each side separately.
- Choose whether the footer and the top navigation bar follow the same margins.
- Small screens always keep the normal margins.

## Theme settings (*Site administration > Appearance > Themes > Flexium*)

- **Branding.**
  - Primary and secondary colours.
  - Colours of the navigation bar, page background, links and footer.
  - A contrast check that warns when text would be hard to read.
- **Typography.**
  - Body and headings fonts.
  - Base font size.
  - Whether Google fonts load from Google Fonts.
- **Layout and shape.**
  - How rounded the corners are.
  - Card shadows.
  - A page background image.
- **Login page.** Background image.
- **Footer.** Footer text, contact email, phone and website, and links to your social networks. The footer blocks use
  them by default.
- **Course catalogue.**
  - Turn the catalogue on or off.
  - Courses per page.
  - Whether the filters panel starts open.
  - The filters, with the same drag-and-drop editor as on the page.
- **Course cards.** Category, summary, badges and their colours, card footer.
- **Home page.** Turn the home page builder on or off. When it is off, Moodle's standard site home page is shown.
- **Catalogue features.** Every catalogue option listed above, in one form.
- **Breadcrumbs.** Every breadcrumb option listed above.
- **Advanced.** Extra SCSS. This is the only place that needs CSS knowledge, and it is optional.

## Per course

Teachers find the *Quick view* field in the course settings, under *Flexium*:
- *Automatic*: the theme decides.
- *Show*: always offer the quick view of the course.
- *Hide*: never offer it.
